Welcome to Spin the Bias

How to read this

The point

This wheel is meant to feel random — like “bad luck” — but the outcomes represent repeatable patterns in data, assumptions, and routines. The cheerful tone is intentional: serious consequences often get normalized.

System damage

A playful score for cumulative harm. Each spin adds damage based on severity — because repeated small biases build large consequences over time.

Trust meter

A rough indicator of confidence in the system. As damage rises, trust drops — reflecting how people lose faith when they’re repeatedly dismissed or misserved.

Understanding the outcome

Each outcome is structured as: why it happens → what goes wrong → real-world impact → what would improve it.

How to use

Spin several times. Notice how “random” starts to look systematic. Tip: Press Space or Enter to spin.
